# Checks for the sharper lower bounds of Part IV (Sections 6, 7 and 8)

Numerical checks of `theory/mass-gap-strong-coupling.tex`, Section "A sharper lower bound: temporal plaquettes at their character weight"
(Lemmas `mg:lem:layerop`, `mg:lem:transfer`, Theorem `mg:thm:sharp`, Corollary `mg:cor:window`, Remark `mg:rem:sharp`). A compass, not
part of the proofs. Run with `python3 <script>` from this directory (needs `numpy`, `mpmath`).

| Script | What it checks | Runtime |
|---|---|---|
| `s3_transfer_bound.py` | $G=S_3$ (two-dimensional representation) on $(\mathbb Z/2)\times$(one square). The single-copy transfer representation used in the proof of Lemma `mg:lem:transfer` agrees with brute-force summation over $6^9$ gauge-fixed configurations (40 random cases, to $10^{-17}$); $\norm{F_f}=u_*$; the duplicated terms $\Phi_F(X)$ for six sets $X$ satisfy the bound of Lemma `mg:lem:transfer`. | ~5 min |
| `fronts_check.py` | Lemmas `mg:lem:fronts` and `mg:lem:incidence` (Section 7; unchanged by the revision) on 400 random sets $X$ without free links outside $S$ (unions and mod-2 sums of tubes and cube boundaries; branched configurations included) on the periodic lattice $8\times5\times5$: vertex degrees in fronts, the column lemma, every segment touched at both ends, adjacency in $X$ reflected in the incidence graph, a path from $S_A$ to $S_B$ covering the inner layers, and $|X|=\sum|\sigma|\ell(\sigma)+\sum|C|$. | ~1 min |
| `local_counts.py` | Edge-rooted constants used by the first version of Section 7 (commit `1766bb3`), by enumeration on spatial tori of dimension 2 and 3 (sides $\ge4$): $D_e=4d-5$, $N_4\le2d-3$, $N_k$ against $e(e(4d-6))^{k-1}$; and $2(d-2)$ plaquettes per link, $K_S=4(2d-5)$, which the revised section still uses. | ~2 min |
| `small_fronts.py` | Lemma `mg:lem:smallfronts`: exhaustive enumeration (Redelmeier) of all connected edge sets of at most six edges through a vertex, on every spatial torus with sides in $\{4,\dots,7\}$ for $d=3,4$; the fronts (minimum degree $\ge2$) number exactly $N^v_4=P_v+n_4$, $N^v_5=n_5$, and $N^v_6=12\binom{d-1}2+96\binom{d-1}3+18(d-2)n_4+n_6$ ($n_j$ = number of sides equal to $j$); $P_v=2(d-1)(d-2)$; $N^v_7$ on large tori against the crude bound. | ~20 s |
| `fronts_constants.py` | Theorem `mg:thm:fronts` (revised): $\lambda_C,\lambda_T$ as the minimal fixed point (iteration from $\lambda_C=0$, the referee's point E1), $\delta=\kappa_C\nu$ in infinite volume ($N^v_4=P_v$, $N^v_5=0$) and finite volume (sides $\ge4$), the loss $8(K+1)\theta_5+\log(1+\delta)$ and the window with the upper bound for $SU(2)$, $d=3,4$ (Remark `mg:rem:fronts`); and Lemma `mg:lem:frontsrange` at $\beta_0(d)$ in the worst case, $3\le d\le12$. | seconds |
| `junctions_small.py` | Lemma `mg:lem:twofront` (Section 8): exhaustive enumeration of the junctions with exactly two front-pairs, an entry of four edges (plaquette boundary or straight cycle) and at most two plaquettes (three for $d=3$), on spatial tori $6\times6$, $7\times7$, $4\times6$, $6\times6\times6$, $4\times6\times6$. With a four-edge exit it finds exactly the cap (one plaquette) and the $8$ ($d=3$) or $32$ ($d=4$) junctions with two plaquettes sharing a vertex; none with three plaquettes ($d=3$); none with a straight entry; no exit at the entry's layer. | ~15 min |
| `junction_structure.py` | Lemmas `mg:lem:junction` and `mg:lem:jgraph` on 400 random admissible sets $X$ (lattice $8\times5\times5$, 2228 junctions): segment ends are front-pairs of exactly one junction, locality, local admissibility $n_J(e)\ge2$, the weight identity, and a junction-graph path from $J_A$ to $J_B$ covering the inner or the outer layers. | ~1 min |
| `junction_constants.py` | Theorem `mg:thm:junctions` and Remark `mg:rem:junctions`: the constants of Definition `mg:def:jconst` (tree-sum exponents, marked constants, $F$) iterated to a solution, $\Sigma_4$ and its parts, $\delta_J$ and the loss for $SU(2)$, $d=3,4$, infinite and finite volume. $\delta_J/b\to1$ as $\beta\to0$; at $\beta_0(4)$ the loss is $4.9\cdot10^{-4}$. Also the worst case over all $G$ and $\rho$ ($u_*=\epsilon$, $\norm{g}_\infty\le e^{4\beta}-1$ or $\gamma_2$) at $\beta_0(d)$, $3\le d\le6$, where the hypotheses still hold (losses $0.051$–$0.70$). | ~1 min |
| `tube_family.py` | Remark `mg:rem:fronts`(iii): the exact sum over the dominant family (one tube with plaquette-boundary fronts, at most three spatial plaquettes per slice, no free links) as a transfer matrix over front positions; its per-layer growth is $a^4(1+b)$ up to $O(b^2)$, an effective $\delta\approx b\approx2\beta$, against $\delta\approx3000\beta$ ($d=3$) proved. | ~10 min |
| `z2_4x4.py` | Exact finite-volume gap of $\mathbb Z_2$ gauge theory, $d=3$, on the $4\times4$ spatial torus (physical space $2^{17}$, Lanczos): $m-4\log(1/u)$ is $-10^{-7}$ at $\beta=0.01$ and smaller below, i.e.\ the true gap is $4\log(1/u)$ up to tiny corrections, as the strong-coupling expansion predicts. | ~5 min |
| `lb_constants.py` | The new lower bound $4\log(1/q_u)$ against the old one $4\log(1/q)$, Part II's $\log(1/(6(d-1)\tanh\beta))$ and the upper bound $4\log(1/u)+D$, for $SU(2)$, $SU(3)$ and $U(1)$ in $d=3,4$, with $u$ computed exactly (Bessel ratios; Weyl integration for $SU(3)$). | ~10 min |

Results on 2026-11-10 are recorded in `journal/2026-11-10.md`. Section 7 was revised the same day after an independent referee report
(accept with minor revisions): vertex-rooted counts, exact small fronts, the closed-polymer ratio bound ($\theta_5$), the sharper sup norm
$\gamma_2$ of $g_p$, and $\delta=\kappa_C\nu$; the old edge-rooted script `local_counts.py` is kept for the record.
