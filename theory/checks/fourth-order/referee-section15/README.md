# Referee checks for Section 15 of Part IV (the fourth-order term in both sectors of charge conjugation)

Written by the referee of Section 15 of `theory/mass-gap-strong-coupling.tex`, 12 November 2026. A compass, not part of the proofs.
The code is independent of `../c_odd_pairing.py`: it builds holonomies from oriented links, computes $\sigma(q,c)$ from Definition 12.1 and
$\epsilon_\Gamma$ from $\partial\partial\Gamma=0$ (`geom.py`), and uses the character tables of `../referee-section14/groups.py` only where stated.
Run each script from this directory; the outputs are in `outputs/`.

| Script | What it checks | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `full_operator_hopturn_z3.py [beta]` | Lemma 15.4 from the definitions, for $\mathbb Z_3$: $G_g$ as a $9\times9$ kernel and $G_g^{1/2}$ by matrix square root, $\Pi_0\otimes\Pi_0$, the explicit gauge projection $P^\Gamma$ (averages at the six vertices, both copies), multiplication by $g_p$, on the full two-copy space of the seven links of $\partial q\cup\partial c$ ($3^{14}$ configurations); the four faces of the turn integrated over the five far links (where every $K$ is $\Pi_0\otimes\Pi_0$ on both sides). $\mathsf C$ acts on link variables, $S$ exchanges the copies. Operators in the basis of indicators of $(U_q,U_c,U'_q,U'_c)$ (spans the range of $P^\Gamma$, checked); no characters. Four geometries ($\sigma=\pm1$, two cubes). | $P^\Gamma$ commutes with the blocks ($2\cdot10^{-15}$); $\lVert Y_{\rm hop}\rVert=u^{12}$ exactly; $Y^0_{\rm turn}=Y_{\rm hop}\mathsf C$ to $6\cdot10^{-17}$; $Q_-(Y_{\rm hop}+Y^0_{\rm turn})Q_-=0$; $\lVert Q_-(Y_{\rm hop}+Y_{\rm turn})Q_-\rVert\le\lVert Y_{\rm turn}-Y^0_{\rm turn}\rVert\le\gamma_gu^{12}$; $\sigma_\Gamma=-\sigma$; the hop kernel is a function of $U_q-\sigma U_c$, the turn kernel of $U_q-\sigma_\Gamma U_c$ (twists $a^\sigma$, $a^{-\sigma}$). Sector norms$/u^{12}$ at $\beta=0.3$: $(S,\mathsf C)=(\pm,-)$: $0.159$ ($=u$: the cap on the $C$-odd state), $(-,+)$: $2.109$, $(+,+)$: $2.210$; at $\beta=1$: $0.547$, $1.960$, $3.150$. So on the $C$-even sectors the norm exceeds $2u^{12}$; the constant $2\varpi$ in step (2) of the proof of Theorem 15.9 should be $(2+\gamma_g)\varpi$. |
| `twists_nonabelian.py` | Lemma 15.4 for non-abelian groups with non-self-conjugate irreps ($F_{21}$, 3-dimensional; $A_4$, 1-dimensional; also $\mathbb Z_5$): exact group sums over one slice (gauge fixed on a spanning tree) for the widening, the narrowing (hexagon oriented as the cycle of $\partial q+\sigma\partial c$) and the cube junction; assembled two-copy blocks. | All 12 cases: widening twist id, narrowing twist $a\mapsto a^\sigma$, turn twist $a\mapsto a^{\sigma_\Gamma}$, $\sigma_\Gamma=-\sigma$; $\lVert Y_{\rm hop}\rVert=u_*^{12}$; $Y^0_{\rm turn}=Y_{\rm hop}\mathsf C$ to $1.3\cdot10^{-14}$. (For $A_4$ the 3-dimensional irrep has $u=0$, as $f_\beta$ factors through $\mathbb Z_3$.) |
| `shared_vertex_toy.py` | The step of Lemma 15.7(ii): $P_v(L\otimes R)P_v=(P^L_vLP^L_v)\otimes(P^R_vRP^R_v)$ when one side of $L$ is $\Pi_0\otimes\Pi_0$ on all its links at the shared vertex. $S_3$ (for abelian $G$ the test is degenerate), two digons sharing a vertex, explicit gauge projections. | With the hypothesis: $1.3\cdot10^{-15}$. Without it (negative control): $4\cdot10^{-2}$. |
| `flip_structure.py` | Definition 15.6 and step (1) of the proof of Theorem 15.9, from the definitions of fronts, clusters, front-pairs and junctions, for explicit sets $X'$ on $\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z^3$ ($d=4$). E1: a turn whose rest has a lid at $y$ at slice $\sigma-1$. E2: a hop whose rest has a lid at $v$ at slice $\sigma$. E3: a hop at $(3,4)$ and, in the rest, a turn $(q',c',5)$ whose entry front passes through $v$. | $X'$ and $X'^*$ have no free links; $\lvert E(X')\triangle E(X'^*)\rvert=10$ (so $\lvert\overline{X'}\triangle\overline{X'^*}\rvert\le20(d-1)\le c_\Gamma$). E1: $X'$ has a turn, $X'^*$ has neither a hop nor a turn in the sense of Definition 15.6 (its junction at $\sigma-1$ has 2 clusters and 3 front-pairs). E2: $X'$ has a hop, $X'^*$ neither. E3: flipping the hop destroys the second move ($(q',c',5)$ is a turn in $X'$, not in $X'^*$). So the partner map is not an involution on sets with moves, and the move vertices (hence $\mathcal J^\natural$) are not invariant under a flip. |
| `u1_values.py` | Corollary 15.10(i): for $U(1)$, $\Upsilon_-=1-I_2/I_0$, $\Upsilon_+=1+I_2/I_0-2(I_1/I_0)^2$. | $\log\Upsilon_--\log\Upsilon_+=\beta^2/4+O(\beta^3)$; $-\log\Upsilon_-=\frac12u^2+\frac7{24}u^4+O(u^6)$, so $-\frac{425}{24}$ ($d=4$) and $-\frac{185}{24}$ ($d=3$); $-(10d-22)/16$. |
