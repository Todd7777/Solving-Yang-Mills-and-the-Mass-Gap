# Second referee of Section 15 of Part IV (the sectors of charge conjugation), revised version

Written by the independent referee of the revised Section 15 of `theory/mass-gap-strong-coupling.tex`, 12 November 2026. A compass, not part of the
proofs. The code does not use `../upsilon_split.py` or `../haar_moments.py`, and assumes no representation theory: the moments of $SU(N)$ come from the
Heine/Toeplitz identity for $U(N)$, summed over the powers of $\det U$ (which turns the $U(N)$ integral into the $SU(N)$ one),
$\langle e^{\alpha\chi+\gamma\bar\chi}\rangle_{SU(N)}=\sum_{q\in\mathbb Z}\det[\hat f_{j-k+q}]_{j,k\le N}$, $\hat f_n$ the Fourier coefficients of $e^{\alpha z+\gamma/z}$.
Run each script from this directory; outputs are in `outputs/`.

| Script | What it checks | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `split_toeplitz_exact.py [DEG]` | Exact rational series (permutation expansion of the Toeplitz determinants with degree pruning) of $\langle e^{\alpha\chi+\gamma\bar\chi}\rangle$ for $SU(2),\dots,SU(9)$ up to total degree `DEG` (default 10; output with 12). From it: the moments $\langle\chi^a\bar\chi^b\rangle$; the exact series of $\Upsilon_\pm$ and of $\Upsilon_+-\Upsilon_-$; the identity $\Upsilon_+-\Upsilon_-=2(\langle\bar\chi^2\rangle_y-\langle\bar\chi\rangle_y^2)$; the Gaussian cancellation (moments $\delta_{ab}a!$). | Moments: Catalan numbers for $SU(2)$ (validates the formula), $\langle\lvert\chi\rvert^{2k}\rangle=k!$ for $k\le N$ ($SU(3)$: $1,1,2,6,23,103,513$), $\langle\chi^N\rangle=1$, zero unless $a\equiv b$ (mod $N$). $\Upsilon_+-\Upsilon_-=\beta^4/248832-\beta^6/179159040+\dots$ ($N=6$), $\beta^5/32269440+\dots$ ($N=7$), $\beta^6/6039797760+\dots$ ($N=8$), leading term $2(\beta/2N)^{N-2}/(N-2)!$ for $3\le N\le9$. $SU(3)$: $\Upsilon_+=1+\frac\beta6-\frac{5\beta^3}{1296}+\dots$, $\Upsilon_-=1-\frac\beta6+\frac{\beta^3}{1296}+\dots$, so $\log\Upsilon_+-\log\Upsilon_-=\frac\beta3-\frac{\beta^3}{648}+O(\beta^4)$. Gaussian moments: $\Upsilon_\pm\equiv1$. |
| `split_numeric_mp.py` | The same generating function at finite $\beta$ (mpmath, 40 digits; derivatives by Cauchy integrals), validated against direct Weyl integration on the maximal torus ($SU(2)$, $SU(3)$); $\Upsilon_+-\Upsilon_-$ for $SU(6)$, $SU(7)$, $SU(8)$ at $\beta=2,1,0.5$ against the exact series; a Monte Carlo compass ($10^7$ Haar samples of $SU(6)$, QR of Ginibre matrices) at $\beta=12$. | Toeplitz = Weyl to $10^{-14}$. Finite-$\beta$ values = series to $10^{-11}$ relative; ratio to the leading term $0.99965$ ($N=6$, $\beta=0.5$), $0.99979$ ($N=7$), $0.99986$ ($N=8$). Monte Carlo at $\beta=12$: $0.0633\pm0.0050$ against the Toeplitz value $0.06783$ ($\Upsilon_+=1.0315$, $\Upsilon_-=0.9636$); also $\langle\lvert\chi\rvert^6\rangle=6.01$, $\langle\chi^6\rangle=1.01$. About 3 minutes. |
| `sector_dressed_tube.py` | Lemma `mg:lem:dressedC` for $\mathbb Z_3,\mathbb Z_4,\mathbb Z_5,\mathbb Z_7$ (two copies, class functions of the plaquette holonomy, $\beta=0.3,0.1,0.03$): $\mathsf C$ commutes with the dressed tube and with $S$; the top eigenvalue $\lambda_C$ on the range of $P_-Q_C$ lies in $[u^4\Upsilon_C,u^4\bar\Upsilon_C]$; the compression to $(e^-_\rho+Ce^-_{\bar\rho})/\sqrt2$ is $u^4\Upsilon_C$. Also the $U(1)$ numbers of Conjecture `mg:conj:codd` and $h_+-h_-=16(d-3)$. | All in the interval; compression exact to $10^{-15}$; $\lambda_C/(u^4\Upsilon_C)-1=O(\beta^6)$. $U(1)$: $-\log\Upsilon_-=\frac12u^2+\frac7{24}u^4+\frac{11}{48}u^6$, so $-\frac{425}{24}$ ($d=4$), $-\frac{185}{24}$ ($d=3$); $h_-u_1^4=(10d-22)/16$. |

What the code does not check: the operator-theoretic steps of Lemma `mg:lem:csectors`, Theorem `mg:thm:sectors` and the derivation of
$m=\min(m_+,m_-)$, which were checked by hand (see the referee report). In particular the code cannot detect the gap reported there in the proof of
Theorem `mg:thm:sectors`(i): the lower-bound machinery of Sections 7–14 bounds covariances of functions of the spatial links of single time slices
only, so the passage to $m_C(\mu_\beta)$ for general local $C$-observables must go through the spectral argument of Theorem `mg:thm:sharp`(ii),
restricted to the $C$-sector of $L^2(\nu)$.
