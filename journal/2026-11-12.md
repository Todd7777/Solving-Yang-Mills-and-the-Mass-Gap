## Part 8: the fourth-order term ("keep going on the fourth-order term")

### Goal
Open thread 1 of Part 7: determine the term of order $\beta^4$ of $m(\mu_\beta)-4\log(1/u)+\log\Upsilon$. After Section 11 this quantity was known to be $O(\beta^4)$,
with $\limsup\beta^{-4}(\dots)\le0$. The exact $\mathbb Z_n$ spectra in $d=3$ suggested a band of width $8u^4$ with its bottom at momentum $0$.

### The answer, found numerically first (commits `9a76d29`, `1e2db46`)
**The plaquette state moves.** Three local processes, each of relative weight exactly $u^4$ for every $G$ and $\rho$ (simple surfaces, every link in two plaquettes):
* **bulge:** the tube on $q$ acquires the unit cube $c\times[s,s+1]$ on a side ($c$ sharing a link with $q$). For one layer the front is the six-edge
  $\partial q\triangle\partial c$; $K_S=4(2d-5)$ of them per unit time.
* **hop:** the same widened layer closed on the other side; the tube continues on $c$.
* **turn / jump** ($d\ge4$): at one slice the four other faces of a unit cube carry the tube from a face $q$ to an adjacent face (turn) or the opposite face (jump).
  These are the $10$ "other-layer" junctions of the referee's $Z_{4,4}=12$. The two "same-layer" ones join two tubes and enter only at order $u^8$.

They define a hopping matrix $H_C$ on the plaquettes, with the orientation sign in the $C$-odd sector:
* coplanar hops and jumps have sign $+1$;
* a perpendicular hop and the turn through the common cube have opposite signs (Lemma 12.2, from $\partial\partial\Gamma=0$).

So $H_+=K_S+A_\parallel+2A_\perp+A_J$ and $H_-=K_S+A_\parallel+A_J$, with tops $h_+=26d-70$ ($8,34,60$ for $d=3,4,5$) and $h_-=10d-22$ ($8,18,28$).

**Conjecture:** $m=4\log(1/u)-\log\Upsilon_C-h_Cu^4+O(\beta^5)$, the band bottom at $k=0$.

**Exact spectra** (`block_spectra.py`, `zn_block_spectra.py`). For every level $m_i$ of the plaquette band, $(m_i-4\log(1/u)+\log\Upsilon_C)/u^4\to-h_i$, with $h_i$ the
eigenvalues of $H_C$ for the same geometry:
* $\mathbb Z_2$ on the $3\times3$ and $4\times4$ tori ($d=3$; all 9 and 16 levels);
* $\mathbb Z_2$ on blocks of $1\times1\times1$, $1\times1\times2$, $1\times2\times2$ cubes ($d=4$, open boundary; all levels), and the top 4 of $1\times2\times3$ ($2^{23}$ states);
* $\mathbb Z_3$ in both sectors on the $3\times3$ torus and the $1\times1\times1$, $1\times1\times2$ blocks. On one cube the $C$-odd band is $5,5,5,3,3,3$, against
  $13,3,3,3,1,1$ for $C$-even: the signs are real.

**Literature.** The model reproduces the classical strong-coupling series. Langelage–Münster–Philipsen, PoS LAT2007:201 (arXiv:0710.0512) eqs. (2.14)–(2.18),
quote Münster (1981) and Seo (1982) for $d=4$. At $k=0$, $h=34$ ($A_1^{++}$), $10$ ($E^{++}$), $18$ ($T_1^{+-}$). With the exact $-\log\Upsilon_C$:

### What was established (Part IV, new Section 12, commit `b51de24`; 70 pages)
* **Definition 12.1, Lemma 12.2:** the hopping matrix, its signs, row sums, $\sup\spec H_C=h_C$, and the $k=0$ blocks ($A_1$ 34, $E$ 10, $T_1$ 18 in $d=4$).
* **Lemma 12.5 (six plaquettes):** a nonempty set of plaquettes in which no link lies in exactly one plaquette has $\ge6$ plaquettes, and $6$ only for a unit cube.
  The proof is extremal: at the top height in a direction $e_k$, only one top plaquette is possible, whose four edges need four side plaquettes, and the bottom
  plaquette closes the cube.
* **Lemma 12.6 (configurations at a slice):**
  * a four-edge front is reached from a plaquette $q'\neq q$ with fewer than five further plaquettes only through a unit cube;
  * a six-edge front is closed with fewer than three only as in a bulge or a hop.
* **Lemma 12.7:** character integrals over one unit cube and over two cubes glued along a face (gluing via the gauge-invariant class function of the shared face).
* **Theorem 12.8:** for every finite set $\mathcal W$ of plaquettes and $a\ne0$,
  $m\le4\log(1/u)-\log\Upsilon_\psi-\log(1+u^4a^\top H_{C,\mathcal W}a/a^\top a)+O(\beta^5)$, with a constant depending on $\mathcal W$; hence
  $\limsup\beta^{-4}(m-4\log(1/u)+\log\Upsilon_\psi)\le-h_Cu_1^4$. Proof:
  * spectral inequality at $t=1$ for $\Phi=\sum a_qA_q$;
  * Lemma 5.2 (positivity), keeping one nonnegative term for each four-edge front $\partial q$ and each six-edge front $\partial q\triangle\partial c$ (the other
    temporal plaquettes of a neighbourhood $E_0$ forced trivial);
  * means subtracted at the one-plaquette mean $m_w$, so that $P_\rho\tilde a=\Upsilon\psi$ exactly and every isolated factor integrates to zero;
  * loop expansion in the gas without the plaquettes over $E_0$, ratios by Lemma 6.1 ($\theta_5$), remainder by Lemma 5.4 after a saturation of the local set;
  * main terms on one or two cubes by Lemma 12.7, with signs;
  * the variance bound with the equal-time cube cross terms $\sigma_\Gamma u^4\Upsilon^2$.
* **Corollary 12.9:** for $SU(N)$, $2\le N\le5$, and $U(1)$, the $\beta^4$ part of $m-4\log(1/u)+\log\Upsilon$ is negative and at most $-h_Cu_1^4$:
  * $-\frac{17}{128}$ for $SU(2)$, $d=4$;
  * $-\frac1{32}$ for $SU(2)$, $d=3$;
  * $-(26d-70)/(16N^8)$ for $SU(N)$, $3\le N\le5$;
  * $-(10d-22)/16$ for $U(1)$, $C$-odd.
* **Conjecture 12.4** with values. In $d=4$:
  * $SU(2)$: $m=-4\log u+2u^2-\frac{98}3u^4=4\log(4/\beta)+\frac7{24}\beta^2-\frac{167}{1152}\beta^4$;
  * $SU(3)$: $-4\log u-3u+9u^2-\frac{27}2u^3-7u^4$;
  * $U(1)$: $-4\log u+\frac12u^2-\frac{425}{24}u^4$.
  In $d=3$, $m+4\log u$ is $2u^2-\frac{20}3u^4$ ($SU(2)$).

## Part 9: the third-order term with an effective constant ("keep going on the third-order term")

### Goal
Section 11 proves $m=4\log(1/u)-\log\Upsilon+O(\beta^4)$, but only asymptotically. The constant of the $O(\beta^4)$ in the lower bound is $\delta'/\beta^4\to8.4\cdot10^{10}$ for
$SU(2)$ in $d=4$ ($3.4\cdot10^8$ in $d=3$). It came almost entirely from the crude count $2\cdot5\cdot(2d-3)\binom{4P_v+4T_v}4$ of the junctions with four plaquettes
and two four-edge front-pairs; the enumerated value $Z_{4,4}=12(d-3)$ was unproved. Aims: prove the counts, identify every junction that enters at order $\beta^4$,
and give the constant in closed form. The same list of $\beta^4$ junctions is what a lower bound at fourth order needs.

### What was established (Part IV, new Section 13: commit `f4f5629`, then the six-edge sum in `134b0b7`; 78 pages)
* **Lemma 13.1 (junctions of order $\beta^4$; Theorem; spatial sides $\ge8$).** For a junction without support, with $A,B$ the fronts of the two layers and $P$ its
  plaquettes, the local condition $n_J(e)\ge2$ gives $A\triangle B\subseteq E(P)$, and every link of $E(P)$ outside $A\cup B$ lies in two plaquettes of $P$. From this:
  * (i) four plaquettes and two four-edge front-pairs: $P\cup\{q,q'\}$ is a unit cube. The proof is a case analysis on whether $q,q'\in P$, using Lemma 5.4
    (five plaquettes) and Lemma 12.5 (six plaquettes). So $Z_{4,4}\le12(d-3)$: 10 turns and jumps and 2 U-turns per four-edge front-pair in $d=4$, none in
    $d=3$, as the Section 11 referee had enumerated.
  * (ii) one plaquette $c$ and a second front-pair with $\ge5$ edges: the front is $\partial q\triangle\partial c$ (6 edges) or $\partial q\cup\partial c$ (7 edges), with $c\sim q$;
    or $\partial q\cup\partial c$ (8 edges), with $c$ sharing one vertex with $q$. The counts are $K_S$, $K_S$ and $4(P_v-1)-2K_S$.
  * (iii) one plaquette and three front-pairs, one of four edges: another has $\ge8$ edges.
  * (iv) two plaquettes and three front-pairs, two of four edges: the third has $\ge6$ edges.
* **Lemma 13.2 (Theorem).** The junction sum $\Sigma^{(4)}_4$ with these counts. For $\ge3$ front-pairs and $n\ge2$ plaquettes, (iv) gives $b^na^E$ with either
  $n\ge3,E\ge2$ or $n=2,E\ge3$. Hence a double majorant $M_4=[(b/\bar b)^3(a/\bar a)^2+(b/\bar b)^2(a/\bar a)^3]\times$(tree sum at $(\bar b,\bar a)$), which is
  $O(\beta^5)$; the bound uses $\min(M_3,M_4)$, with $M_3$ the Section 11 majorant.
* **Lemma 13.3 (Theorem; added second, `134b0b7`).** The junction sum at a six-edge front-pair is $\Sigma^{(6)}=2b+O(b^2)$. The $2b$ are the two
  narrowings of $\partial q\triangle\partial c$: by the plaquette $c$ back to $\partial q$ (a bulge), or by $q$ to $\partial c$ (a hop). The proof: a domino boundary has
  exactly one chord, the common link, which determines $\{q,c\}$. So $f(6)$ gets its own constant $F_6\sim b/18$, in place of $F\sim\frac{13}5P_v^2b$. This
  modifies two induction steps of Theorem 10.8: a third entry in $M$, and the hypothesis $\Sigma^{(6)}<36F_6e^{6\xi_F}(\bar\Upsilon(1+\delta')-a)$.
* **Theorem 13.4 (Theorem).** The third-order lower bound holds with $\delta'=(\delta'_4+o(1))\beta^4$, where
  $\delta'_4=8(4P_v-4-K_S)(2-\iota)N^2u_1^2+192(d-3)+8K_Su_1^2$, $u_1=\frac{1+\iota}{2N^2}$. The three terms are the moves of the tube at the sup norm:
  * the shifts and jumps, through $\varpi\min(b^2,b_2)$;
  * the cube moves (turns, jumps, U-turns), $12(d-3)b^4$;
  * the bulges and hops, $K_Sbf(6)\sim2K_Sb^2a^2$.

  The first version (`f4f5629`, with the crude $F$) had $\frac{1872}5K_SP_v^2u_1^2$ for the last term. Values (fundamental representations; first version in
  brackets):

  | | $d=3$ | $d=4$ |
  |---|---|---|
  | $SU(2)$ | 18 (1514) | 262 (40691) |
  | $SU(3)$ | 3.65 (77.5) | 206.5 (2203) |
  | $U(1)$ | 40 (6022) | 344 (162061) |

  The limit is about $3\cdot10^8$ times smaller than Section 11's for $SU(2)$, $d=4$.
* **Window for the $\beta^4$ coefficient (with Corollary 12.9).** For $SU(2)$, $d=4$, $\beta^{-4}(m-4\log(1/u)+\log\Upsilon)$ has $\liminf\ge-262$ and
  $\limsup\le-\frac{17}{128}$. In $d=3$ the window is $[-18,-\frac1{32}]$.

### What failed / limitations
* **The improvement is asymptotic.** At practical $\beta$ the terms of order $\beta^5$ with crude counts dominate:
  * the junctions with $\ge5$ plaquettes, constant about $2\cdot10^{13}$ in $d=4$;
  * the majorant $M_4$, about $2\cdot10^{16}$.

  $\delta'/\beta^4$ comes within a factor 2 of $\delta'_4$ only for $\beta\lesssim10^{-14}$ ($d=4$) and $10^{-11}$ ($d=3$). At $\beta=10^{-4}$ to $10^{-5}$ the gain over
  Section 11 is a factor 8 to 30. The tree sum of $M_4$ scales like $\bar b\,\bar a^{3.7}$ in the tested range, not $\bar b^2\bar a^2$. It is dominated by trees with one plaquette,
  which the junctions it bounds do not have, and by the crude $N^v_k$, $k\ge7$: $N^v_7\le3.9\cdot10^8$, against exactly $\frac34P_vK_S=108$ (parity). The exact
  $N^v_6=132$ and $N^v_7=108$ alone gain only a factor 3 in $M_4$.
* **The double majorant alone is worse than Section 11's for $\beta\gtrsim10^{-6}$ ($d=4$).** The tree sums do not know that $n=2$ forces $E\ge3$, so they contain
  "phantom" terms $\bar b^2\bar a^2$, which the prefactor turns into $b^3a^2/\bar b$. Hence $\min(M_3,M_4)$.
* **$\delta'_4=262$ is still about $2\cdot10^3$ times the conjectured coefficient $\frac{17}{128}$.** There are two sources, now that the six-edge count is exact:
  * the sup norm $b\approx2\beta$ in place of characters ($u=\beta/4$ for $SU(2)$): a factor $8^4$ on the cube moves (192 against $12u_1^4$), and $64$ on the bulges
    and hops;
  * the shifts and jumps (64), which vanish on the odd sector but are still charged.
* **A slow first run.** The first effective run recomputed the tree constants at every $(\bar b,\bar a)$ in every iteration; caching them made it fast.
