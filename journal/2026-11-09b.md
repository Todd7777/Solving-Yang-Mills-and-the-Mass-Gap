# Session 3 — 2026-11-09 (evening)

## Goal

The user asked to continue on the matching upper bound for the strong-coupling mass gap (open thread 2 of
`journal/2026-11-09.md`): prove $m\le4\log(1/u(\beta))+O(1)$, so that together with Part IV's lower bound the
strong-coupling gap is pinned to $4\log(1/\beta)+O(1)$.

## What was established

### New Section 5 of Part IV (`theory/mass-gap-strong-coupling.tex`, now 13 pages)
For $d\ge3$, $\rho$ irreducible of dimension $N$ ($\iota=1$ if $\rho\cong\bar\rho$, else $0$), $u=c_\rho/(Nc_0)$:

* **Exact leading term** (Lemma `mg:lem:leading`). For $A=\mathrm{Re}\,\chi_\rho(U_{p_0})$ ($p_0$ a spatial plaquette at
  time 0) and $A_1$ its translate at time 1: $\tau_0:=\int A^2=\frac{1+\iota}2$, and the four sides of the unit cube between
  them give exactly $\tau_1:=\int AA_1\prod_{\rm sides}f_\beta=\tau_0u^4$ (character orthogonality on the cube, using the
  computation in [F]'s cube-activity proposition); in the duplicated expansion this set contributes exactly $2\tau_1$.
  Explicit bounds $\frac{(1+\iota)\beta e^{-\beta}}{2N^2}\le u\le\frac{(1+\iota)\beta+\beta^2e^\beta}{2N^2}$.
* **Unique minimal connecting set** (Lemma `mg:lem:minimal`): the four sides are the only connecting set with at most four
  plaquettes and non-zero contribution, on every torus with sides $\ge3$ (including the girth-3 case).
* **Two-point bounds** (Lemma `mg:lem:twopoint`): $\mathrm{Cov}(A,A_1)\ge\tau_1(e^{-48(d-1)\theta}-\delta(\beta))$ and
  $\mathrm{Var}(A)\le\tau_0(1+O(\beta))$, uniformly in the volume.
* **Upper bound** (Theorem `mg:thm:upper`): $\mathrm{Cov}\le e^{-m}\mathrm{Var}$ for the ground-state chain (Part II in
  infinite volume; [F]'s ground-state formalism in finite volume) gives $m\le4\log(1/u)+D(\beta)$, $D=O(\beta)$, for the
  infinite-volume theory and for every spatial lattice with sides $\ge3$, whenever $\delta(\beta)<e^{-48(d-1)\theta}$.
* **Two-sided asymptotics** (Corollary `mg:cor:twosided`): $m(\mu_\beta)=4\log(1/\beta)+O(1)$ as $\beta\downarrow0$,
  explicitly $-4\log(4e^3K)+O(\beta)\le m-4\log(1/\beta)\le4\log\frac{2N^2}{1+\iota}+O(\beta)$; the same for finite-volume
  gaps with spatial sides $\ge4$. With this the strong-coupling endpoint passes principle 3 of the research program in full.

### Checks

* Referee: see below.

## What failed / limitations
* **The threshold is absurdly small**: the upper bound is proved for $\beta\lesssim5\cdot10^{-17}$ ($SU(2)$, $d=4$),
  $\lesssim3\cdot10^{-20}$ ($SU(3)$). Cause: the leading term carries the dimension-reduced weight $u\approx(1+\iota)\beta/(2N^2)$
  per face, while the remainder is bounded with sup-norms ($\approx4\beta$ per plaquette) and polymer counting ($\approx eK$
  per plaquette). The asymptotic statement is rigorous; the numbers are not physically meaningful.
* A character-expansion bound for the remainder (each face of a closed surface weighted by $\approx u$) would fix this,
  but general plaquette sets are branched surfaces, for which the clean manifold formula $d_r^{\chi-F}$ does not apply.
  Not attempted.
