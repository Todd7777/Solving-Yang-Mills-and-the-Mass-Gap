## Part 19: the third-order term of SU(5) ("keep going on the third-order term")

By Corollary 11.6 the gap of $SU(N)$ has a third-order term only for $N=3$ ($\beta^3/432$) and $N=5$ ($-\beta^3/6000$, about 14 times smaller). The counts of Section 16 do not depend on
the group, so $SU(5)$ needs only its $u$, $\Upsilon$ and a bound for $u_2$.

**Established.**
1. *Group data from exact Haar moments* (`sun_group.py`; Numerical, exact rational coefficients).
   * By Schur–Weyl duality, $\int\chi^a\bar\chi^b=\sum f^\lambda f^\mu$ over partitions with at most $N$ rows that give the same $SU(N)$ representation.
   * So $u$, $U_\pm$ and $\Upsilon$ are power series in $\beta$, evaluated at any precision (with extra digits at small $\beta$, where $\Upsilon-1=O(\beta^3)$).
   * They agree with `second_order_constants.su2`/`su3` to 40 digits.
   * The series reproduce Corollary 11.6: $\beta^2/8$ for $SU(2)$; $-\beta/6+\beta^2/72+\beta^3/432$ for $SU(3)$; $-\beta^2/128$ for $SU(4)$; $-\beta^3/6000$ for $SU(5)$; $O(\beta^4)$ for $SU(6)$.
2. *Lemma 16.11 (Theorem), the second character coefficient of $SU(N)$.*
   * With $c_0\ge1$ (Jensen), $d_ru_r\le\sum_{a,b}t^{a+b}m_r(a,b)/(a!b!)$.
   * The order-2 multiplicities are exact: $\mathrm{Sym}^2$, $\Lambda^2$ and $\mathrm{ad}$, each once. The rest is at most $R/d_r$, with $R=e^\beta-1-\beta-\beta^2/2$.
   * For $SU(5)$, $u_2\le\beta^2/2000+R/100$, about $10^{-3}$ times the old $e^\beta-1-\beta$.
   * It enters $\bar\Upsilon$ through $n_B\approx(u_2/u)^2\epsilon$. With the old bound, $\log(\bar\Upsilon/\Upsilon)=0.254\beta^4$ at $\beta_0(3)$, which alone sinks $d=3$.
3. *Lower bound, $d=3$* ($S''_9$, $\theta$ of (vii)).
   * $D(\beta_0(3))=0.345\beta^4$ against $0.414\beta^4$. The parts: majorant $0.279$, shifts and jumps $0.036$, $\theta$-loss $0.027$, $\log(\bar\Upsilon/\Upsilon)$ $3\cdot10^{-13}$.
   * The scaling passes at all 17 values of $t$: $D\le0.3454\beta^4$ for all $\beta\le\beta_0(3)$.
   * The monotonicity facts hold for $SU(5)$. The one change: $\bar\Upsilon-a=1-\beta/50+\dots$ decreases, so the right sides of the $F$-conditions only grow as $t\to0$.
4. *Lower bound, $d=4$.*
   * Without changes, $D=1.48\beta^4$ against $1.084\beta^4$; the corner $(9,2)$ binds.
   * Only the class $4,4,4$ has pairs with $E=2$, since $E=(k-4)+(\sum K-k)/2$. With nine plaquettes it has $148894$ junctions (`junctions.c`, 18 s; the partition search of the
     sixth and seventh referees, 6 s, the same).
   * With $S^\times_{10}\cup\{(9,2)\}$ (`orders/d4_s10x_92.json`), the optimiser moves to $\bar a=2^{-10}$ and the majorant drops from $1.05$ to $0.086$. $D(\beta_0(4))=0.519\beta^4$.
   * The scaling passes: $D\le0.519\beta^4$.
5. *Upper bound* (`upper_exact.py --group 5`; $N^4/\tau_0^2=2500$ instead of 324).
   * $d=3$ with the counts to 12: $E(\beta_0(3))=0.3178\beta^4<0.414\beta^4$, and $E/\beta^5$ increases.
   * $d=4$ with the counts to 10: $1.59\beta^4$, because the crude tail beyond 10 plaquettes ($5.4\cdot10^{-4}\beta^4$ of $\mathcal T'_Q$) is multiplied by 2500.
   * The admissible sets of 11 plaquettes in $\mathbb Z^4$: $760$ for $S_A$, $2616$ for $S_Q$ ($624$ with two components). They were counted by the fifth referee's Redelmeier code,
     split into independent first-level branches (`admissible_split.c`; the pieces reproduce every count to 10; $S_A$ in 4 pieces, $S_Q$ in 16, about an hour on 4 cores).
   * A container restart killed the first attempt, a single run of an estimated 2–4 hours. The pieces are resumable (`run_admissible_pieces.sh`).
   * With them $E(\beta_0(4))=0.2511\beta^4$, and $E/\beta^5$ increases. The values do not matter (the same up to $10^9$); what matters is that the crude tail then starts at 12.
   * For $SU(3)$ the same counts give $E(\beta_0(4))=0.0327\beta^4$ (was $0.207$).
6. **Result** (Numerical, infinite volume). For $SU(5)$, $-0.346\beta^4\le m-4\log(1/u)+\log\Upsilon\le0.318\beta^4$ in $d=3$ and $-0.519\beta^4\le\dots\le0.252\beta^4$ in $d=4$, for all
   $\beta<\beta_0(d)$. So the term $-\beta^3/6000$ is determined from both sides on the whole range. The two groups $SU(N)$ with a third-order term are now both done in $d=3$ and $d=4$.
7. *$SU(3)$, $d=4$, with $(9,2)$ and the sets of 11 plaquettes:* $D(\beta_0(4))=3.93\beta^4$ (was $11.38$), and the scaling gives $D\le3.94\beta^4$. The residual is $26\%$ of the term instead of
   $76\%$, as the seventh referee suggested. With the upper side: $-3.94\beta^4\le m-4\log(1/u)+\log\Upsilon\le0.033\beta^4$.
8. *Tools.*
   * `--group N` in `practical_beta.py`, `scaling_check.py`, `monotone_check.py` and `upper_exact.py`; the default stays $SU(3)$ as refereed.
   * `second_order_constants.dressed` takes `g['u2']` when the group data give it.
